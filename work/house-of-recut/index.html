House of Recut

Preserving generations of memories through AI storytelling

Sustainable fashion · Vadodara, Gujarat, India · 2026

01 / Context

About the business

House of Recut reimagines treasured family garments, including grandparents' wedding and traditional attire, into outfits for meaningful modern occasions.

02 / Objective

The challenge

The new concept needed original premium AI video narratives with visual consistency across each reel.

03 / Approach

Our strategy

We combined prompt engineering, AI fashion models, product visuals and creative direction to express the emotional value of heritage garments. Each reel explored a different story rather than repeating a single promotional formula.

04 / Impact

Results and impact

The distinctive concept and consistent advertising-style visuals attracted attention in Vadodara and Gujarat's fashion community.

Results are reported by DigiBuilder and do not guarantee future performance.
