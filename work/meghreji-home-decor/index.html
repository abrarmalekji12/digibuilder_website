Meghreji Home Decor

Making natural cladding a recognisable regional brand

Architecture & home decor · Gujarat, India · 2022–present

01 / Context

About the business

Meghreji Home Decor supplies and installs natural cladding and interior and exterior elevation materials for homes and buildings.

02 / Objective

The challenge

The established business wanted to introduce expanded offerings and refresh its brand for architects, engineers, builders and homeowners.

03 / Approach

Our strategy

Showroom reels, product photography, installation progress and real building transformations demonstrated materials in context. Before-and-after storytelling made their benefits easier to understand.

04 / Impact

Results and impact

Content gained viral local visibility in Modasa and Gujarat. The business reported projects across Gujarat, supported by growing recognition for its natural cladding offerings.

Results are reported by DigiBuilder and do not guarantee future performance.
