01 / Context

About the business

Sharp Immigration helps students, families and professionals explore international immigration and study options across Canada, India and Nigeria.

02 / Objective

The challenge

Restricted Meta advertising access and a banned page challenged the brand's ability to reach relevant audiences across three markets.

03 / Approach

Our strategy

We supported recovery of advertising access, developed informative content and aligned campaigns with students, parents and entrepreneurs. Campaign direction was reviewed every 15 days using performance feedback.

04 / Impact

Results and impact

The project reported over seven million organic views, including one reel exceeding six million views, and over 900 monthly leads during the reported campaign period.

Results are reported by DigiBuilder and do not guarantee future performance.
