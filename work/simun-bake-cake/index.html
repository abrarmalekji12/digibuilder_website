Simun Bake & Cake (Simun Tea)

Taking a local bakery to a nationwide marketplace

Bakery & FMCG · Modasa, Gujarat, India · 2025–present

30+

Organic Amazon orders in first month

No PPC

Amazon launch

01 / Context

About the business

Simun Bake & Cake is a premium bakery in Modasa, offering cookies, toast, rusk and a newer tea offering under Simun Tea.

02 / Objective

The challenge

The business needed to launch its FMCG products digitally and generate sales without an established Amazon history. Diwali gift hampers offered a separate regional opportunity.

03 / Approach

Our strategy

We supported Amazon listings and organic marketplace visibility, alongside food photography, reels and a festive gifting campaign. Meta advertising helped generate gift hamper enquiries.

04 / Impact

Results and impact

The brand reported 30+ organic Amazon orders in its first month without Amazon PPC. The Diwali campaign gained visibility in Modasa and generated sales and enquiries across Gujarat.

Results are reported by DigiBuilder and do not guarantee future performance.
