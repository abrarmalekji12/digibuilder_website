Scholar Career Academy

Connecting students with academic opportunities

NEET & JEE education · Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India

01 / Context

About the business

Scholar Career Academy is an Ahmedabad-based coaching institute for NEET and JEE aspirants.

02 / Objective

The challenge

The academy wanted stronger local visibility and student registrations, including for its Talent Hunt examination.

03 / Approach

Our strategy

Institute reels, teacher-led videos, academic programme coverage and graphic design highlighted the learning environment and exam opportunities.

04 / Impact

Results and impact

The content gained viral visibility in Ahmedabad and Gujarat, supporting student registrations and awareness of the Talent Hunt exam.

Results are reported by DigiBuilder and do not guarantee future performance.

Scope of work

  • Institute reels
  • Teacher videos
  • Creative graphics
  • Talent Hunt promotion
  • Programme coverage
  • Student registration campaigns
