Saathi4u

Launching an AI-powered matchmaking brand

Matchmaking & technology · India, Canada & USA · 2024–present

10K+

Registrations in first year

01 / Context

About the business

Saathi4u is an AI-based matchmaking platform focused on NRI audiences and people seeking meaningful relationships across India and North America.

02 / Objective

The challenge

From the initial idea, the business needed a brand identity, digital platform and launch strategy that could earn trust in an established category.

03 / Approach

Our strategy

We supported the brand from its early concept through website and app launch. Content and advertising focused on relevance, privacy-conscious communication and awareness among prospective members.

04 / Impact

Results and impact

The platform reported over 10,000 registrations within its first year, with viral visibility in Gujarat and Canada.

Results are reported by DigiBuilder and do not guarantee future performance.
